Five ways to see every session at once

The board locked each card into a stage. The open canvas freed them, but with a dozen cards nothing stood out. Each layout below gives the page a structure that comes from something always true about a session, instead of a stage it can drift out of. All five use the same sessions. Press Play changes to have them come and go on their own: finishing turns, asking for things, failing. Then watch how each layout copes.

Whose move it is

Every session is either waiting on you, being worked by Claude, or parked. That flips all the time, and it's always true. All five layouts key off it, not the stage.

Unread turns

A turn that finished while you were elsewhere counts as unread, as in mail. It's the quiet kind of "needs you" the canvas missed.

How long it has waited

A session that has been blocked on you for 14 minutes matters more than one that asked 10 seconds ago. Waiting time is shown and used for ordering.

Trying it is a step

"Ready to try" is something a session needs from you, with Start, Open, Looks good and Found a problem. A problem goes back to Claude as a message.

Sessions
Keys work in the mock: ↑ ↓ or j k move, a goes to the next that needs you, y n allow or deny, 1–3 answer, t start the app, l looks good, p found a problem, s mark as seen, r retry.
