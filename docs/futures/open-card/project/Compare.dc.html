The open card · what the three directions share
All three keep the new-session conventions: the header reads back-button, ticket key, stage, title; eyebrow headings; the Esc keycap in the corner; few keycaps on screen, the legend and ? for the rest; the message box under the transcript; tabs gone. They differ in what the eye lands on first and where the facts go.

A recommendation
Start with A. It keeps the two-column shape people already know, it is the smallest change, and it fixes the real complaint: the page shows everything at once with no order of importance. B’s rail and C’s sticky “now” strip can be added to A later without undoing it. Do C only if the owner finds themselves reconstructing what happened from the Context and Transcript tabs.
Where the conventions may bend
The new-session popup is a form: top to bottom once. The open card is a place people return to while something runs, so one thing is allowed that the popup never has: a live, pinned “now” (the ask, or what Claude is doing) that stays in view while the rest scrolls. The transcript keeps its own scroll, pinned to the newest line unless you scrolled up.
