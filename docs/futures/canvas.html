What was decided (2026-10-08)

  • Stable order: newest first, and a card stays where you saw it. Needing you shows as an amber tile and the count in the bar. a jumps to the next card that needs you. < > or a drag moves a card earlier or later.
  • Tickets to start sit in a strip above the grid. i folds it, v switches between your tickets and Ready for QA, and n or Enter starts one.
  • The quick view follows the focus. With it open, ↓ ↓ ↓ looks through each session in turn.
  • The workspace repo row is gone. A chip's tooltip and the workspace dialog E show the repos. + − ⇧E ⇧I F still work.
  • "Lane" becomes "Workspace" everywhere.

For you to check in this mock

  • Keys in the quick view: Enter still opens the card fully, as it does on the canvas. r (reply) puts you in the message box, and works on the canvas too. In the open card, Enter is still the message box.
  • Esc in the quick view only closes it. It doesn't stop Claude, as Esc does in the open card, because a fast look through the sessions shouldn't stop one by accident.
  • Digits: with a question in the quick view, 1–9 pick an answer. Otherwise they pick the workspace, as they do now.
  • The stage strip (Plan · Build · Try · Ship) on each tile: is it worth its space?
